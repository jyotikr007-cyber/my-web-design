Tribute to A.P.J Abdul Kalam

About Kalam


A.P.J.Abdul Kalam, the Missile Man Of India, was born on October 15th,1931 in Rameshawaram,Tamil Nadu.
He served as the 11thPresident of India in 25th July ,2002 till 25th July 2007,and was a renowed
scientist.Known for his work, India's space and missile programs.He worked in ISRO and DRDO before electing as a politician.

Achievements

  • developed India's first Sateltite launch vechile(SLV-III)
  • Led India's successful nuclear test in 1998
  • Wrote books like "Wings Of Fire","India 2020"
  • Received the Bharat Ratna, India's highest Civilian award in 1997
